Company profile

Our story

Plaksha Industries Pvt. Ltd. was established in the year 2020. It was a revolutionary venture in the area of the Healthcare Sector.

Our products are Finished Dosages (Tablets, Capsules, Syrups and Ointments) and Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients, Intermediates and Key Raw Material.

Our offered products are manufactured using optimum quality raw material with international quality standards by a team of highly qualified professionals. We make sure our products are available to customers at leading prices in the market and within the stipulated time period.
